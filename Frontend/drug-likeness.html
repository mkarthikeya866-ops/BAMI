💡 Scientific Note on Terminology: Christopher Lipinski formulated the "Rule of Five" because all 4 core physicochemical cutoff thresholds are multiples of 5: Molecular Weight ≤ 500 Da (5 × 10²), LogP ≤ 5 (5 × 1), Hydrogen Bond Donors ≤ 5 (5 × 1), and Hydrogen Bond Acceptors ≤ 10 (5 × 2). Rotatable bonds (≤ 10) and TPSA (≤ 140 Å²) are part of Veber's criteria and are reported under General Molecular Descriptors.
