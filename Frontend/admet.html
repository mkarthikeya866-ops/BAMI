      Model Architecture & Held-Out Test Evaluation

      All 10 endpoint models were trained independently with scikit-learn Random Forests utilizing 2048-bit Morgan Fingerprints (radius 2, chiral) + 10 physicochemical descriptors, validated on strict 20% held-out test splits without data leakage.

      ⚠️ Methodological Limitations & Preclinical Guidance

      Computational ADMET predictions are statistical approximations derived from public experimental assays. They are intended to prioritize chemical series and flag toxicological liabilities during early lead discovery. Predictions do not account for high-dose metabolic saturation, idiosyncratic drug reactions, transporter-mediated efflux (e.g., P-gp), or patient-specific genetic polymorphisms. All computational findings must be verified using in vitro assays (such as PAMPA, Caco-2, human liver microsomes, and patch-clamp hERG assays) prior to clinical evaluation.
